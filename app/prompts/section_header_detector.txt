You are analyzing a resume. Each non-empty line is prefixed with its index: "[N] text".

List EVERY section header in the resume, in the order they appear, then label each one.

A section header is a short standalone line that introduces the block of content below it.
Recognise it by structure, not by wording:
- short, usually under six words, with no sentence punctuation
- visually set apart: ALL CAPS, Title Case, or on a line of its own
- it is NOT a job title, company, school, project name, date range, or bullet
- the lines that follow it are the content it introduces

Never skip a header because its wording is unusual. "PROJECTS", "RELATED PROJECTS",
"SIDE PROJECTS" and "WHAT I HAVE BUILT" are all headers and must all be listed.

For each header report:
- "index": the index of the header line itself, not the first content line
- "text":  the header line copied verbatim
- "kind":  which section it introduces, from this list

  "summary"    profile summary, objective, about me
  "experience" paid employment, internships, work history
  "education"  degrees, schools, coursework
  "projects"   personal, academic, or side projects
  "skills"     technical skills, tools, languages
  "other"      genuinely fits none of the above (awards, publications,
               certifications, volunteering, interests)

Use "other" only as a last resort. If a header plausibly introduces one of the five known
sections, label it with that kind even when the wording differs from the examples above.
